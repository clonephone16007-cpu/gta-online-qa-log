## BUG-008 — Casino heist big con: Gruppe Sechs service door blocks you on entry

**game:** GTA Online
**platform:** PC, PS4, PS5
**build:** v1.50+
**logged:** September 2024
**severity:** SEV-3 | **priority:** P3 | **status:** OPEN

### what's happening

Gruppe Sechs entry door has a collision issue during its opening animation. If you walk through too quickly after triggering the interaction you hit the door while it's still swinging open — it physically blocks you even though visually it's opening. Sometimes you clip into it slightly and get briefly stuck.

Not a big deal. Backing up and retrying usually gets you through in 1-2 attempts. Just looks broken and wastes a few seconds.

### how to see it

1. Big Con, Gruppe Sechs approach
2. trigger the service entrance door
3. immediately try to walk through as the animation starts
4. either bounce off the door or clip slightly into it

waiting a beat after triggering before moving reduces it a lot.

### workaround

don't rush through it. trigger the door, wait ~1 second, then walk. almost never clips if you do that.
